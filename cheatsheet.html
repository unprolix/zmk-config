jjb keymap — what to remember

Two halves. Sections 1–8 drill the things you keep forgetting; section 9 is the picture of every layer on the Rolio and the eyelash, generated from their keymaps by just cheatsheet, so it cannot drift from the firmware. Open the front half when you want to break a habit, the back half when you want to look something up.

1 — Modifiers, three flavors

Same modifier, three ways to invoke. Pick the one that fits the moment.

You want…Hold-style (intuitive)Sticky-combo (when hold misfires)
CTRL+<key>hold A (left ring)LH0+LH1 thumb pair — primary one-shot
SHIFT+<key>hold E (left middle)tap an arcane thumb — see below
ALT+<key>hold I (left index-adjacent)E+A home row
GUI+<key>hold the inner thumb (under SPACE)LH1+LH2 thumb pair
CTRL+SHIFT+<key>(awkward — two same-hand HRMs fight prior-idle)H+I adjacent home row — for terminal copy/paste
Hold a home-row mod without using it and it stays armed for the next key, up to five seconds — CTRL+X can be hold A, let go, press X. Holding it down across several keys works too; the modifier is live either way. Anything you press consumes it.
If your CTRL keypress is misfiring during fast typing, switch to the thumb-pair combo LH0+LH1. It's a single-press one-shot CTRL — no HRM timing involved. Same for GUI: LH1+LH2.

Right hand mirrors all of it: S = RCTRL, T = RSHIFT, R = RALT, N = RGUI+nav, and every combo above exists on the right home row and right thumbs too.

What it does otherwise — the arcane key has two different fallbacks, and mixing them up is why it sometimes surprises you:
  • Letter with no entry in the table (say S then right thumb) → &key_repeat, i.e. it types that letter again. Not a shift.
  • Anything that isn't a letter, or more than 650 ms since the last one → shift, counted by taps: 1 = sticky shift · 2 = caps_word (ends at the next word boundary) · 3 = caps lock and the pink CAPS layer, which either arcane thumb turns off again. Tap-then-type has no wait; only stopping at two taps waits out the 300 ms term.
If you find yourself spelling out a word that's in the table, you forgot. Drill the ones matching your most-typed words — this is the page that fixes "I'm not using these as much as I should".

5 — Stop typing things you don't have to

Adaptive keys (no extra keypress — the key changes with what came before)

  • After U, the Q key produces K — the rare K-after-U bigram without reusing the same finger. (After Q, it repeats instead.)
  • Type . then J and you get ../ — J emits ./ when a dot precedes it.
  • After N, the H key produces 't — ca, n, H gives can't. After I it gives a bare ', so I, H, d gives I'd. Neither NH nor IH occurs in English, which is what makes the key free to do this.
  • On SYMBOL: after ~, the ` key produces /.
Comma then Y gives you ", ". Y sits next to the comma and becomes a space when a comma has just been typed, so the pair is a roll along the bottom row. Comma on its own is only a comma.

Single-fire macro combos

  • RB4+RB5 (.+/) → ../
  • RB1+RB4 (C+. diagonal) → .com
  • LB5+RB5 (outer bottom corners) → switch to QWERTY
  • LM3+RB2 = "I'm " · LM3+RT3 = "I'll " · LM3+RT2 = "I'd " · LM3+RB0 = "I've " (all are E plus one right-hand key)

Mod-morphs (shift the punctuation)

  • shift + ' → -  ·  shift + " → _
  • shift + . → :  ·  shift + , → ;
  • em-dash via 3-key combo LB2+LB1+LB0 or RM3+RM4+RM5

7 — The lights mean something

Eyelash. Six LEDs per half, one per column — the hardware has no per-key control. The column that lights tells you what the keyboard currently thinks it is doing. The Rolio and the Corne v4 light per key, which this section does not describe.

WhenWhat lights
A modifier is held (base layer)its own column: ctrl shift alt gui
SYMBOL / NAVIGATION / NUMPAD heldone column on the holding hand — numpad also puts cyan on the other hand
FUNCTIONouter two columns, both halves, red
EXTRAoutermost cyan, next one in violet
GAMINGcolumn 3 only, dim white
LIGHTINGcolumns 3 and 4, white at two weights — the columns the lighting keys are in
BLUETOOTHsee below — this one is live data, not a fixed colour
The bluetooth layer reads out your profiles. Hold it and each profile takes the column its own BT key is in:
  • Left half — green: connected · blue: paired but not connected · dark: never paired
  • Right half — white marks the profile currently selected
The nice!view says the same thing in words while you're on the layer: >2 idle means profile 2 is selected and paired but asleep.

Brightness and on/off live on the LIGHTING layer (leader LED). Eight levels; the eyelash's encoder rotates through them, and the setting is saved, so it survives a reboot. Dimming stops at the dimmest lit level — use the toggle to go dark.

8 — When typing feels wrong, try this

"thisi s" instead of "this is" — you rolled the SPACE-thumb into 'i'. The hold-tap on the inner thumb defers the SPACE until release. Either separate space from the next letter deliberately, or move to the thumb-pair GUI combos (LH1+LH2 / RH1+RH2) so the inner thumb can be demoted to a bare SPACE.

shift+' didn't produce " — you used the same-hand shift HRM (E) for a left-hand key. Same-hand HRMs don't fire. Use the opposite hand's shift, or tap an arcane thumb for a sticky shift first.

HRM modifier didn't fire — the previous keypress was within HRM_PRIOR_IDLE (150 ms), which disables the HRM and forces a tap. Pause briefly before the modifier-letter, or fall through to the sticky combos (LH0+LH1 CTRL, LH1+LH2 GUI).

A sticky modifier did nothing — pause about 150 ms first, then hold past the tapping term and release without touching anything else. Miss any of those and you get the letter. Five seconds then to press the target.

FUNCTION or EXTRA armed when you didn't mean it — those two have no idle guard, so they can arm mid-word. A same-hand roll is safe (for, fun), but a cross-hand one is not: f then a right-hand key can arm FUNCTION, which makes fry the word to watch.

The arcane key shifted when you wanted a word — more than 650 ms passed since the letter, so it fell through to sticky-shift. Or the letter has no entry and you got it twice.

9 — Every layer

Every layer twice: the Rolio, then the eyelash corne. Generated by just cheatsheet from config/rolio.keymap and config/eyelash_corne.keymap; rerun it after any keymap change. The Corne v4 and the Toucan run the same layout, and differ only in keys outside the shared block. Small text in a corner is the hold behaviour; the main text is the tap.

Hierophant (base)

Home. Everything else returns here with the inner-thumb pair LH0+RH0.

QWERTY

For someone else at your keyboard, or software that assumes it. LB5+RB5 (outer bottom corners) to enter.

Symbol

Hold U or D. Tap LH2 for a sticky one-key version.

Numpad

Hold Q or W, or double-tap either outer thumb for numpad_word, which stays until you type something that isn't numpad — space or enter ends it. The home row keeps its mods and the top row its layer keys (pinky FUNCTION, ring EXTRA, middle SYMBOL); holding any of them leaves NUMPAD first, so Ctrl+Z is Z, not 7, and nothing that needs a held shift can be typed here. Thumbs: 0 on both outer thumbs; left middle ., right middle ⌫, either middle held is NAV. Rolio only: ( ) on the left extra pair; = and a silent way out on the right.

Numeric

Hold P or G; tap RH2 for sticky; LM2+LM4 or RM2+RM4 also gives one sticky key.

Navigation

Cross-thumb combo LH1+RH1, or hold a middle thumb.

LGUI + nav

Hold H (left pinky home). Same layer plus LGUI held — window management.

RGUI + nav

Hold N (right pinky home). The mirror of the above.

Extra

Hold Z or L.

Function

Hold F or B — sticky, so it arms the layer for one following press. F1–F24. Reset and bootloader live on SYSTEM, out of reach of a stray press.

Superscript

Leader SUPER toggles it. Home-row mods survive here on purpose (hml_sup/hmr_sup), so holding still gives you a modifier while tapping gives a superscript.

Empty 2

A placeholder. Nothing reaches it and every key is transparent; it holds the index so the layers numbered after it keep theirs, which rolio.keymap and toucan.keymap share.

Gaming

Leader GAME. Column 3 lights dim white so you can tell at a glance. No thumb is a hold-tap. Digits come from a one-shot NUMPAD on the left middle thumb, the right inner thumb, or the outer key of the left home row. Esc is the combo R+T, Caps is F+G.

Lighting

Leader LED. Lights on/off, brighter and dimmer; on the eyelash that is eight steps, and its encoder turns them too.

Bluetooth

Leader BT. Top row selects a profile, home row disconnects one, outer bottom-left clears the current pairing and the far pinky clears them all. USB/BLE output on the right top row.

System

Leader SYS, and only that: it carries reset and bootloader, so there is no chord a palm could find. Mirrored on each half — same finger, either hand. Either inner thumb leaves.

All combos

Every combo on one picture. The ones worth drilling are in sections 1 and 3 above.
