Tap-dances

  • caps_word_lock_dance — 1: sk LSHIFT, 2: caps_word, 3: CAPS_LOCK
  • num_dance / smart_num_lock_dance — 1: num_word NUM, 2: sl NUM, 3: rgb-color marker
  • menu_layer / sqt_layer — quote/menu key tap fires char, second tap activates num_word NUM
  • thumb space-tab / space-ret — hold = LGUI/RGUI; tap = SPACE; double-tap = TAB / RETURN

Quick lookup: emacs / terminal pain points

  • ALT-/ — entering SYMBOL: hold LM2 (LALT), tap RM3 (/).
  • CTRL-= — entering SYMBOL: hold RM1 (RCTRL), tap LT4 (=).
  • CTRL-− — entering SYMBOL: hold LM4 (LCTRL), tap RM2 (−).
  • CTRL-+ — entering SYMBOL: hold RM1 + a sticky shift (combo LM2+LM3 or RM2+RM3) + tap LT4.
  • CTRL-SHIFT-C / V — combo LM1+LM3 (or RM1+RM3) → next key gets Ctrl+Shift. Then C or V.
